Safety Warning

Danger - High Voltage

Many appliances contain high voltage components that can cause serious injury or death. Specifically:

  • Microwave ovens: Contain capacitors that store lethal voltage up to 5000 volts even when unplugged
  • Air conditioners: Have high voltage connections and moving parts
  • Refrigerators: Contain pressurized refrigerant and high voltage
  • Washing machines: Combine water and electricity, a dangerous combination
  • Televisions: Power supplies can hold dangerous charges
  • Inverters: High voltage DC and battery acid hazards

If you are not qualified and experienced, do not attempt internal repairs. Always call a certified technician for complex issues.
