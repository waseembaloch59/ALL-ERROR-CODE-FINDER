Cooling Problems and Solutions

Refrigerator Not Cooling

Symptoms: The fridge is warm, the freezer is soft, the compressor may be running or not.

Solutions: Clean the condenser coils at the back or bottom with a brush or vacuum. Check if the evaporator fan in the freezer is running. Ensure the door seals are tight with no warm air entry. If the compressor is hot but not running, the start relay may be bad. If all checks pass, the refrigerant may be low. Call a technician.

Refrigerator Freezing Food

Symptoms: Vegetables are frozen, ice forms in the fridge section.

Solutions: Adjust the thermostat to a warmer setting. It should be 2 to 3 degrees Celsius. Check the air damper at the top of the fridge. It should move freely. Test the temperature sensor resistance. Replace the sensor if the readings are incorrect.

Water Leaking from Refrigerator

Symptoms: Water puddle under the fridge or inside the fridge.

Solutions: Clear the defrost drain with hot water mixed with vinegar. Use a turkey baster to flush the drain tube. Check the door gasket for tears or gaps. Ensure the refrigerator is level. The front should be slightly higher than the back. Check water line connections if you have an ice maker.

E3 Error - Defrost System

Diagnosis: Check if the freezer has ice buildup. Test the defrost heater with a multimeter. It should have continuity.

Fix: Replace the defrost heater if the circuit is open. Also check the defrost thermostat and timer.

Compressor Not Running

Diagnosis: Listen for a hum. If it is humming but not starting, check the start relay and overload protector.

Fix: Replace the start relay. This is a small black box on the compressor. If the compressor clicks but will not start, the capacitor may be bad.

Maintenance Tip: Clean condenser coils every 6 months. In dusty Pakistani cities, clean every 3 to 4 months. This simple step can extend refrigerator life by years and reduce electricity bills by 10 to 15 percent.

Safety Warning: Refrigerators contain high voltage components and refrigerant under pressure. Never attempt to open the sealed system. For compressor issues, refrigerant leaks, or major electrical problems, always call a certified technician.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my refrigerator running but not cold?
Dirty condenser coils are the number one cause. Clean the coils with a brush. Also check if the evaporator fan is running. Open the freezer door, press the door switch, and see if the fan runs.
How often should I defrost my refrigerator?
For manual defrost models, defrost when frost exceeds 1/4 inch, about every 4 to 6 months. Frost free models auto defrost, but may need manual defrost if the system fails.
What temperature should my refrigerator be?
The fridge should be 2 to 4 degrees Celsius. The freezer should be minus 18 degrees Celsius. Use an appliance thermometer to check accuracy.
Why is water leaking from my fridge?
A clogged defrost drain is the most common cause. Pour hot water mixed with vinegar down the drain tube to clear it. Also check if the fridge is level.
How long does a refrigerator last?
The average lifespan is 10 to 15 years. Regular maintenance like coil cleaning and door seal checks extends the life.

Pakistan Specific Tips: In areas with voltage fluctuations, use a voltage stabilizer to protect the compressor and PCB. Clean condenser coils more often in dusty cities like Karachi and Lahore. During load shedding, keep the fridge door closed to maintain temperature. For professional service, use authorized service centers for your brand.
